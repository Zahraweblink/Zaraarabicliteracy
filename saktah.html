Tajweed & Recitation Mechanics

Saktah Studio (السَّكْتَة)

Master Saktah: a brief 2-count silent pause on the symbol (س) without taking a new breath.

Execution Rules of Saktah

2 Counts 1. Duration

Hold the sound briefly for approximately 2 counts (the length of a regular Harakah movement).

NO Breath 2. Airflow

Do not break or replenish your breath during the pause; hold the remaining air.

س 3. Quranic Notation

Indicated by a small letter Seen (س) placed above the word in Hafs 'an 'Aasim.
