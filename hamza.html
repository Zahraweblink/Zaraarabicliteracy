Hamzatul Wasl & Qat' Studio (همزة الوصل والقطع)

Comprehensive mastery of the two types of Hamzah: the Connecting Hamzah (Wasl) which drops when continuing, and the Cutting Hamzah (Qat') which is always pronounced.

Key Differences & Examples at a Glance:

أَرْسَلْنَا Hamzatul Qat' (Cutting)

Always pronounced whether starting or continuing speech. Carries a written Hamzah symbol (ء). Found in nouns, verbs (past 4-letter), and particles.

ٱهْدِنَا Hamzatul Wasl (Connecting)

Pronounced only when starting. Skipped entirely when connecting from a previous word. Carries a small Saad symbol (صـ) or bare alif.
