Tajweed Phonetics & Articulation Mechanics

As-Sifāt Studio (صِفَات الحُرُوف)

Master As-Sifāt: Whisper (Hams), Strength (Shiddah), Softness (Rikhawah), and Stickiness (Itbāq).

Core Letter Characteristics

فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتَ الهَمْس (Hams / Whisper)

Flow of breath due to weakness in articulation when pronouncing letters.

أَجِدُ قَطْ بَكَتْ الشِّدَّة (Shiddah / Strength)

Complete blockage of sound flow due to strong reliance on the articulation point.

س، ش، ز الرَّخَاوَة (Rikhawah / Softness)

Free flow of sound through the articulation point when pronouncing soft letters.

ص، ض، ط، ظ الإِطْبَاق (Itbāq / Stickiness)

Flattening and raising a large portion of the tongue toward the roof of the mouth.
