Comprehensive Module Breakdown

Detailed Tajweed principles governing a silent Mīm (مْ) meeting various Arabic letters.

إِخْفَاءُ شَفَوِيّ Ikhfā' Shafawī

1. Labial Concealment (ب)

Rule: When a silent Mīm (مْ) is followed by the letter ب, the Mīm is concealed with Ghunnah (nasal sound lasting 2 vowel counts).

Example:

تَرْمِيهِمْ بِحِجَارَةٍ

Termihim bi-hijarah (Conceal Mīm at 'bim')

إِدْغَامُ مُتَمَاثِلَيْنِ Idghām Shafawī

2. Labial Assimilation (م)

Rule: When a silent Mīm (مْ) is followed by a moving Mīm (م), they merge into one emphasized Mīm with complete Ghunnah.

Example:

لَهُمْ مَا يَشْتَهُونَ

Lahum-ma yashtahoon (Merged Mīms)

إِظْهَارٌ شَفَوِيّ Iẓhār Shafawī

3. Labial Clarity (Other Letters)

Rule: When a silent Mīm (مْ) meets any letter other than ب or م, it is pronounced clearly without extra nasal elongation.

Example:

أَمْ لَمْ تُنْذِرْهُمْ

Am lam tundirhum (Clear Mīm articulation)
