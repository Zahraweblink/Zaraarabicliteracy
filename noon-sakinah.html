1. Izhar (إِظْهَار) - Clarity Trigger Letters: ء , هـ , ع , ح , غ , خ
Definition: Pronouncing the Noon Sakinah (نْ) or Tanween clearly and distinctly from its articulation point without any extra nasal sound (Ghunnah). This occurs when followed by any of the 6 throat letters.
