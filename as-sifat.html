Comprehensive Letter Characteristics & Examples

فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتَ الهَمْس (Hams / Whisper)

Flow of breath due to weakness in reliance on the articulation point. Letters: ف, ح, ث, ه, ش, خ, ص, س, ك, ت.

أَجِدُ قَطْ بَكَتْ الشِّدَّة (Shiddah / Strength)

Complete blockage of sound flow due to extreme strength. Letters: أ, ج, د, ق, ط, ب, ك, ت.

س، ش، ز، خ، ذ، ث الرَّخَاوَة (Rikhawah / Softness)

Free flow of sound through the articulation point when pronouncing soft friction letters.

ص، ض، ط، ظ الإِطْبَاق (Itbāq / Stickiness)

Flattening and raising a large portion of the tongue toward the roof of the mouth, producing heavy emphasis.
