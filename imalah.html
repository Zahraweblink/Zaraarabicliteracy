Mechanics & Occurrence in Hafs 'an 'Aasim

مَجْر۪ىٰهَا 1. Sole Location

Occurs in only one word in Hafs recitation: Surah Hud (11:41).

Fathah ➔ Kasrah 2. Vocal Shift

The vowel sound tilts halfway between "A" (Fathah) and "E/I" (Kasrah), sounding like "Maj-ray-ha".

◊ or ۪ 3. Mus-haf Notation

Marked by a solid diamond symbol (◊) under the letter Raa (ر۪).
