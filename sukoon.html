Sukoon Station (السُّكُون)

Master the zero-vowel resting mark. Sukoon stops the vowel flow, linking the resting letter directly to the vowel before it.

How Sukoon Works:

ـْ The Symbol

A small circle sitting on top of a letter.

No Vowel Resting Sound

No "A", "I", or "U" sound is added to the letter.

أَبْ Joint Reading

Alif + Fatha + Ba Sukoon = "AB"
