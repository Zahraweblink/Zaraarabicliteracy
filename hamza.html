Hamzatul Wasl & Qat' (همزة الوصل والقطع)

Master the two types of Hamzah: the Connecting Hamzah (Wasl) which drops when continuing, and the Cutting Hamzah (Qat') which is always pronounced.

Key Differences at a Glance:

أَرْسَلْنَا Hamzatul Qat' (Cutting)

Always pronounced whether starting or continuing speech. Carries a written Hamzah symbol (ء).

ٱهْدِنَا Hamzatul Wasl (Connecting)

Pronounced only when starting. Skipped entirely when connecting from a previous word. Carries a small Saad symbol (صـ).
