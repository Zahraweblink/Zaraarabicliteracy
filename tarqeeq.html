When does Tarqeeq (Lightness) apply?

حُرُوفُ الاِسْتِفَال 1. Always Light Letters

21 letters outside heavy خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ (e.g. ب, ت, س, ك, م).

رِ / رْ (مَكْسُور) 2. Light Raa (ر)

When carrying Kasra (رِ) or preceded by Kasra (فِرْعَوْن).

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ 3. Light Lam (ل)

Lam in Allah becomes light when preceded by Kasra.
