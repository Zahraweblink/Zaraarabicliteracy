Stopping & Pausing Signs (رُمُوزُ الأَوْقَافِ)

Master the rules of stopping (Waqf) to preserve meaning and recite with precision.

Key Stopping Signs Reference

مـ Mandatory Stop

Stopping is compulsory to prevent distorting meaning.

قلي Preferred Stop

Stopping is better, though continuing is allowed.

صلي Preferred Continuation

Continuing is better, though stopping is allowed.

لا Forbidden Stop

Do not stop; sentence structure requires continuing.

∴ ∴ Paired / Linked Stops

Stop at one of the two places, but not at both.
