Rules of Lam & Ra (أَحْكَامُ اللَّامِ وَالرَّاءِ)

The letters Lam (ل) and Ra (ر) fluctuate between heavy sound elevation (Tafkhīm / تَفْخِيم) and light sound thinning (Tarqīq / تَرْقِيق) based on surrounding vowels.

Core Dynamic Rules (تَفْخِيم وَتَرْقِيق): اللَّه • رَ • رُ • رِ (Heavy with Fat-ḥah/Ḍammah • Light with Kasrah)
1. Rules of Lam in The Majesty Name (َلَامُ لَفْظِ الجَلَالَة)

Heavy Lam Jalālah (تَفْخِيم لَامِ اللَّه)

Condition: When the letter immediately preceding the name Allāh (اللّٰه) or Allāhumma (اللّٰهُمَّ) carries a Fat-ḥah ( َ ) or a Ḍammah ( ُ ).
Example: قَالَ اللَّهُ , نَصْرُ اللَّهِ.

Light Lam Jalālah (تَرْقِيق لَامِ اللَّه)

Condition: When the letter immediately preceding the name Allāh carries a Kasrah ( ِ ).
Example: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ , قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ.

2. Rules of Ra (أَحْكَامُ الرَّاءِ)

Heavy Ra (تَفْخِيم الرَّاء)

• Ra carries Fat-ḥah (رَ) or Ḍammah (رُ).
• Ra is Sukūn (رْ) preceded by Fat-ḥah or Ḍammah.
• Ra is Sukūn preceded by temporary Hamzatul-Waṣl.
• Ra is Sukūn preceded by Kasrah, BUT followed by a heavy letter (حَرْفُ اسْتِعْلَاء) in the same word (e.g. فِرْقَةٍ).

Light Ra (تَرْقِيق الرَّاء)

• Ra carries Kasrah (رِ).
• Ra is Sukūn (رْ) preceded by an original Kasrah inside the same word, and NOT followed by a heavy letter.
• Ra on Pause (Stopping) preceded by Ya Leen / Ya Madd (e.g. قَدِيرٌ , خَيْرٌ).

⚠️ Common Pronunciation Errors:

• Over-rolling: Repeating the tongue tip excessively (Takreer / تَكْرِير) when pronouncing heavy Ra.
• Lightening Lam Jalālah after Fat-ḥah: Pronouncing قَالَ اللَّهُ with a thin flat "L" sound instead of a deep full-mouth tone.
