The 5 Primary Exit Zones (Al-Makhaarij Al-Asliyyah)

1. الجَوْف (Al-Jawf)

The empty space of the mouth and throat. Exit point for the 3 Madd letters (ا، و، ي).

2. الحَلْق (Al-Halq)

The throat. Divided into 3 sections for the Hamza, Ha, Ain, Haa, Ghain, and Kha.

3. اللِّسَان (Al-Lisan)

The tongue. The largest zone containing 10 separate exit points for 18 letters.

4. الشَّفَتَان (Ash-Shafatan)

The lips. Exit zone for Fa, Meem, Ba, and Waaw.

5. الخَيْشُوم (Al-Khayshoom)

The nasal cavity. The exclusive exit source for Ghunnah (nasal sound accompanying Noon and Meem).
