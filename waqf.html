Stopping & Pausing Signs (رُمُوزُ الأَوْقَافِ)

Comprehensive Masterclass: Complete module breakdowns, phonetic voice synthesis, and rigorous practice featuring 40 curated test questions.

Comprehensive Module Breakdown

Detailed linguistic principles, definitions, and reciter guidelines for Rumūz al-Awqāf.

مـ Waqf Lazim

1. Mandatory Stop (الوقف اللازم)

Explanation: A compulsory pause where stopping is required because continuing would alter, corrupt, or invert the intended Qur'anic meaning.

Example Verse:

إِنَّمَا يَسْتَجِيبُ الَّذِينَ يَسْمَعُونَ مـ وَالْمَوْتَىٰ

Stop at "يَسْمَعُونَ" to complete the affirmative statement before mentioning the dead.

قلي Al-Waqf Awla

2. Preferred Stop (الوقف أولى)

Explanation: Stopping is superior and preferred here, though continuing without taking a breath is grammatically permissible.

Example Verse:

مَّن ذَا الَّذِي يُقْرِضُ اللَّهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا فَيُضَاعِفَهُ لَهُ قلي

Pausing here allows the listener to digest the rhetorical question before the reward clause.

صلي Al-Wasl Awla

3. Preferred Continuation (الوصل أولى)

Explanation: Continuing the recitation without stopping is superior and preferred, though pausing is permitted if out of breath.

Example Verse:

وَإِن يَمْسَسْكَ اللَّهُ بِضُرٍّ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُ إِلَّا هُوَ صلي

Keeping the recitation flowing preserves the indivisible attribution of power to Allah.

لا Forbidden Stop

4. Forbidden Stop (الوقف الممنوع)

Explanation: Stopping here is strictly prohibited because the sentence is incomplete and requires immediate continuation to maintain sense.

Example Verse:

طَيِّبِينَ لا يَقُولُونَ سَلَامٌ عَلَيْكُمُ

Do not pause at "طَيِّبِينَ" because "يَقُولُونَ" is the direct adjectival state of the angels.

∴ ∴ Mu'anaqah (Embracing Pause)

5. Paired / Linked Stops (المعناقة)

Explanation: Two sets of triple dots placed close together. The rule dictates that the reciter must stop at one of the two marked positions, but stopping at both or skipping both is incorrect.

Example Verse:

ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ ∴ فِيهِ ∴ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ

Pause at either "رَيْبَ" or "فِيهِ", but not both consecutively.
