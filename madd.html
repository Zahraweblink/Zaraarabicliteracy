Madd Mastery Studio (أَحْكَامُ الْمَدِّ)

Madd (Elongation) means lengthening the sound of a Harf al-Madd (Mad letter) by a specific duration measured in counts (Harakāt).

The 3 Mad Letters (حُرُوفُ الْمَدِّ): ا • و • ي (Alif preceded by Fatha • Wāw Sakin preceded by Dhamma • Yā’ Sakin preceded by Kasra)
1. Primary Categories of Madd
• Madd Aṣlī / Ṭabī‘ī (Natural Madd): Stretched for 2 counts. Has no Hamza or Sukun following it (e.g., قَالَ).
• Madd Muttaṣil (Connected Madd): Mad letter is followed by a Hamza in the same word. Stretched for 4–5 counts (e.g., جَاءَ).
• Madd Munfaṣil (Separated Madd): Mad letter is at the end of a word, and Hamza is at the start of the next word. Stretched for 4–5 counts (e.g., فِي أَنْفُسِكُمْ).
• Madd ‘Āriḍ li-s-Sukun (Temporary Sukun Madd): Occurs when pausing on a word where a temporary Sukun is placed on the last letter after a Mad letter. Stretched for 2, 4, or 6 counts (e.g., الْعَالَمِينَ).
• Madd Lāzim (Obligatory Madd): Mad letter is followed by an authentic Sukun or Shaddah in the same word. Always stretched for 6 counts (e.g., الضَّالِّينَ).

⚠️ Common Recitation Errors & Master Guidance:

  • Inconsistent Counts: Shortening Madd Muttaṣil or expanding Madd Ṭabī‘ī beyond 2 counts. Keep your timing rhythmic!
  • Ignoring Temporary Sukun: Failing to maintain duration when stopping at the end of an ayah for Madd ‘Āriḍ.
