Heavy & Light Letters (التَّفْخِيم وَالتَّرْقِيق)

Master Tafkheem (full, heavy echo produced by elevating the back tongue) and Tarqeeq (flat, light tone).

Core Rules of Tafkheem & Tarqeeq:

خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ 🔥 Always Heavy (7 Letters)

خ ، ص ، ض ، غ ، ط ، ق ، ظ
Always pronounced heavy regardless of vowels.

ر / اللَّه ⚖️ Conditionally Heavy/Light

Lam in Allah: Heavy after Fatha/Damma; Light after Kasra.
Raa (ر): Heavy with Fatha/Damma; Light with Kasra.
