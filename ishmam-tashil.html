الإِشْمَام (Ishmām) Example: Surah Yusuf (12:11) - لَا تَأْمَنَّا

Definition & Mechanism: Pointing the lips outward into a Dammah formation instantly after pronouncing the silent Noon Sukoon without producing a vocalized vowel sound. It visually communicates the underlying grammar without altering the acoustic count.

التَّسْهِيل (Tashīl) Example: Surah Fussilat (41:44) - أَ۠عْجَمِيٌّ

Definition & Mechanism: Softening the second consecutive Hamzah so that it falls midway between a harsh glottal stop (Hamzah) and a soft elongating vowel (Alif), avoiding vocal fatigue or unnatural harshness.
