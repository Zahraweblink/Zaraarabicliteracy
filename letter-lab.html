Arabic Letter Lab (حُرُوفُ الهِجَاءِ)

Mastering the 28 Arabic letters requires understanding their Articulation Points (Makhārij), Heavy/Light qualities, and Positional Shapes inside words.

The 7 Heavy / Full-Mouth Letters (خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ): خ • ص • ض • غ • ط • ق • ظ (Pronounced with back of tongue raised to palate)
1. Key Articulation Zones (الْمَخَارِجُ)
• Throat (الْحَلْق): 6 Letters — ء , هـ , ع , ح , غ , خ
• Tongue (اللِّسَان): 18 Letters — ق , ك , ج , ش , ي , ض , ل , ن , ر , ط , د , ت , ص , ز , س , ظ , ذ , ث
• Lips (الشَّفَتَان): 4 Letters — ف , ب , م , و
2. Positional Forms (Connecting vs. Non-Connecting)

Most letters connect on both sides. However, 6 Non-Connecting Letters connect only to the letter preceding them, but never to the letter after them:

أ • د • ذ • ر • ز • و

⚠️ Common Pronunciation Errors:

  • Confusing Similar Sounds: Mixing heavy ط with light ت, or heavy ص with light س.
  • Throat Articulation: Substituting ح (deep throat) with soft English "H" (هـ).
