Understanding Madd Rules (أَحْكَامُ المَدَّ)

Madd (أَلْمَدّ) means prolonging or stretching the voice when pronouncing one of the three Madd letters: Alif (ا), Waw (و), or Ya (ي).

The 3 Letters of Madd (حُرُوفُ المَدّ): نُوحِيهَا (ا • و • ي) (Alif preceded by Fat-ḥah • Waw preceded by Ḍammah • Ya preceded by Kasrah)
Core Madd Classifications

1. Madd Aṣlī / Ṭabī‘ī (مَدّ أَصْلِي / طَبِيعِي) — Natural Madd

Duration: Exactly 2 counts.
Natural stretching inherent to the letter without any Hamzah or Sukūn following it.

2. Madd Muttaṣil (مَدّ مُتَّصِل) — Connected Obligatory Madd

Duration: 4 or 5 counts.
Occurs when a Madd letter is followed by a Hamzah (ء) inside the SAME single word.

3. Madd Munfaṣil (مَدّ مُنْفَصِل) — Disconnected Permissible Madd

Duration: 4 or 5 counts (2 allowed in specific riwāyāt).
Occurs when the Madd letter ends one word, and the Hamzah (ء) begins the NEXT word.

4. Madd ‘Āriḍ li-s-Sukūn (مَدّ عَارِض لِلسُّكُون) — Presented Sukūn Madd

Duration: 2, 4, or 6 counts.
Occurs when stopping at the end of an Āyah creates a temporary Sukūn right after a Madd letter.

5. Madd Lāzim (مَدّ لازِم) — Compulsory Madd

Duration: Strictly 6 counts.
Occurs when a original (permanent) Sukūn or Shaddah directly follows a Madd letter.

⚠️ Key Distinctions to Remember:

• Muttaṣil vs. Munfaṣil: Look at the word boundary! جَاءَ (Same word = Muttaṣil) vs. إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ (Two words = Munfaṣil).
• Madd Lāzim is always held for a full 6 counts regardless of continuous reading or stopping!
