Ishmām & Tashīl Studio (الإِشْمَام وَالتَّسْهِيل)

Master Ishmām (lip-rounding without sound) and Tashīl (softening the second Hamzah) in Hafs 'an 'Aasim.

Articulation Rules & Mechanisms

الإِشْمَام (Ishmām) Surah Yusuf (12:11) - لَا تَأْمَنَّا

Rounding the lips (as if making a Dammah) during the silent Sukoon of Noon without uttering any sound.

التَّسْهِيل (Tashīl) Surah Fussilat (41:44) - أَ۠عْجَمِيٌّ

Softening the second Hamzah between a pure Hamzah and an Alif, avoiding a harsh glottal stop sound.
