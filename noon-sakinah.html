Understanding Nūn Sakinah & Tanween (أَحْكَامُ النُّونِ السَّاكِنَةِ وَالتَّنْوِينِ)

When a Nūn Sakinah (نْ) or Tanween (ً ٍ ٌ) meets any of the 28 Arabic letters, it produces one of four fundamental Tajweed rules:

The 4 Rules of Nūn Sakinah & Tanween: إِظْهَار • إِدْغَام • إِقْلَاب • إِخْفَاء (Clarity • Merging • Conversion • Concealment)
Rule Summary

1. Izhar Halqi (إِظْهَار حَلْقِي) — Absolute Clarity

6 Throat Letters: ء, هـ, ع, ح, غ, خ
Pronounce the Nūn Sakinah or Tanween clearly from its origin without extra nasal stretching.

2. Idgham (إِدْغَام) — Merging

6 Letters (يَرْمَلُونَ): ي, ر, م, ل, و, ن
• With Ghunnah (2 counts): ي, ن, م, و (YAMNW)
• Without Ghunnah: ر, ل (R, L)

3. Iqlab (إِقْلَاب) — Conversion

1 Letter: ب (Baa)
Convert the Nūn sound into a hidden Mīm (م) accompanied by a 2-count Ghunnah.

4. Ikhfa' Haqiqi (إِخْفَاء حَقِيقِي) — Concealment

Remaining 15 Letters: ت, ث, ج, د, ذ, ز, س, ش, ص, ض, ط, ظ, ف, ق, ك
Conceal the Nūn tongue placement while sustaining nasal resonance for 2 counts.

⚠️ Single-Word Exception (Izhar Mutlaq):

When Nūn Sakinah is followed by an Idgham letter (و or ي) within the SAME single word, Idgham is forbidden! You must apply Izhar Mutlaq (Absolute Clarity). There are only 4 occurrences in the entire Quran:
الدُّنْيَا • بُنْيَانٌ • قِنْوَانٌ • صِنْوَانٌ
