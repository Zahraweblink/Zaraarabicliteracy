The 5 Primary Exit Zones (Al-Makhaarij Al-Asliyyah)

1. الجَوْف (Al-Jawf)

Empty mouth and throat space. Exit point for the 3 Madd letters (ا، و، ي).

2. الحَلْق (Al-Halq)

The throat. Divided into 3 deep, middle, and upper sections for 6 letters.

3. اللِّسَان (Al-Lisan)

The tongue. The largest zone containing 10 separate exit points for 18 letters.

4. الشَّفَتَان (Ash-Shafatan)

The lips. Exit zone for Fa, Meem, Ba, and Waaw sounds.

5. الخَيْشُوم (Al-Khayshoom)

The nasal cavity. The exclusive exit source for Ghunnah resonance.
