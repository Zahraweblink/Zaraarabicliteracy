Tajweed Phonetics & Articulation Mechanics

As-Sifāt Studio (صِفَات الحُرُوف)

Master As-Sifāt: Whisper (Hams), Strength (Shiddah), Softness (Rikhawah), and Stickiness (Itbāq).

Core Letter Characteristics

الهَمْس (Hams / Whisper)

Flow of breath due to weakness in articulation when pronouncing letters (e.g., فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتَ).

الشِّدَّة (Shiddah / Strength)

Complete blockage of sound flow due to strong reliance on the articulation point (e.g., أَجِدُ قَطْ بَكَتْ).

الرَّخَاوَة (Rikhawah / Softness)

Free flow of sound through the articulation point when pronouncing soft letters (e.g., س, ش, ز).

الإِطْبَاق (Itbāq / Stickiness)

Flattening and raising a large portion of the tongue toward the roof of the mouth (ص, ض, ط, ظ).
