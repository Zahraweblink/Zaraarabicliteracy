Rules of Sukoon (سُكُون):

أَبْ 🔗 Static Sound Blending

A letter with a Sukoon (ْ) attaches directly to the letter before it for clean consonantal closure.

أَحَدْ ⭐ Qalqalah Bounce (قطب جد)

Produce a distinct echo or bouncing sound when stopping on Qalqalah letters without adding a full vowel.
