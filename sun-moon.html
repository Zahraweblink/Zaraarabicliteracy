Sun & Moon Letters (الحُرُوفُ الشَّمْسِيَّة وَالقَمَرِيَّة)

Master how the definite article AL (الـ) behaves before the 28 Arabic letters.

Rules of "AL" (الـ):

الشَّمْس (Ash-Shams) ☀️ Sun Letters (14 Letters)

The "L" sound is silent and merges into the next letter, creating a Shaddah.
Example: ال + شَمْس = الشَّمْس ("Ash-Shams")

الْقَمَر (Al-Qamar) 🌙 Moon Letters (14 Letters)

The "L" sound is clear with a Sukoon (الْـ).
Example: ال + قَمَر = الْقَمَر ("Al-Qamar")
