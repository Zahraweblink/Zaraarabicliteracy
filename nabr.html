Nabr Studio (النَّبْر)

Master Nabr: applying a subtle acoustic pressure/lift to ensure certain letters are not dropped or slurred during recitation.

Primary Situations Requiring Nabr

الصَّاخَّةُ 1. Madd to Shaddah

Lifting the voice when transitioning from long Madd (6 counts) into a doubled letter (Madd Laazim).

مُسْتَقِرٌّ 2. Stopping on Shaddah

Applying pressure when stopping on a doubled letter so the second hidden letter remains audible.

ذَاقَا الشَّجَرَةَ 3. Dropped Alif of Dual

Lifting the sound on the previous letter when the Alif of dual is dropped due to meeting another Sukoon.
